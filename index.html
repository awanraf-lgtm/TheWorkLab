Generative artificial intelligence

Generative AI automates content creation and information extraction, freeing your teams from repetitive, low-value-added tasks.

What generative AI enables:

  • Improve team efficiency

    Optimize teams’ work by integrating conversational agents into internal documentation to respond quickly to their needs.

  • Increase company productivity

    Automate data extraction from various sources (PDF, E-mail, Text, Images) to increase efficiency and free up time.

  • Optimize information management

    Centralize information and quickly find the documents you need thanks to an intelligent search.

  • Accelerate content creation

    Generate and send bids, tenders or legal documents faster, while maintaining high quality of work.

  • Improving call center performance

    Increase call center automation and efficiency with voice transcription and automated response generation.

  • Optimize repetitive processes

    Reduce time spent on time-consuming tasks by integrating advanced audio, photo and video recognition systems to automate manual processes.

Agentic artificial intelligence

Agentic AI enables the automation of entire processes through agents that can plan, execute, and adapt their actions autonomously—while interacting intelligently with your existing systems.

What Agentic AI can do:

  • Autonomously perform tasks

    Delegate complex processes to agents that can act without constant supervision, while respecting defined goals and constraints.

  • Automate complex end-to-end workflows

    Coordinate multiple steps that require contextual decisions—such as information gathering, data analysis, and deliverable production.

  • Interact with existing IT systems

    Use standard user interfaces (internal applications, CRMs, databases) or integrate with existing systems via APIs, depending on your automation needs.

  • Maintain memory and long-term context

    Follow long-term objectives while considering priorities, business rules, and constraints specific to your environment.

  • Collaborate with human teams

    Act as a virtual teammate—able to ask questions, adapt to project needs, and proactively suggest actions.

  • Orchestrate multi-step business processes

    Oversee the sequence of operations involving multiple departments or systems, adapting to intermediate results to achieve the desired outcomes.
